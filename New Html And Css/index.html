About HEIM

Run like a construction site, finished like an interior studio.

Ahmed Hesham founded HEIM in 2022, after five years leading finishing crews and site supervision on residential builds across New Cairo and the North Coast.

He is a civil engineer by training — Bachelor of Science, Helwan University, graduated Very Good with Honors — and a certified Project Management Professional (PMP, 2023). In practice, that means a written schedule before work starts, materials confirmed before they're ordered, and one person accountable for the site from first day to handover.

HEIM takes on a small number of villas and apartments at a time, in Cairo and for Egyptian clients based abroad, and covers each project end to end: 2D technical drawings, 3D visualisation, material sourcing, and direct site supervision through to handover.

How a project runs

Five steps, in order, every time.

01

Consultation & brief

A walk-through of the space or the drawings, and a plain conversation about budget, scope and timeline.

02

Design & technical drawings

2D layouts covering plumbing, electrical, ceilings and lighting, plus 3D visuals so you see the result before work starts.

03

Material selection

Marble, wood, fittings and finishes sourced and confirmed with you before anything is ordered.

04

Execution & supervision

Ahmed supervises the site directly through every phase — not a rotating team of subcontractors.

05

Handover

A final walkthrough and sign-off, on the date agreed at the start.
