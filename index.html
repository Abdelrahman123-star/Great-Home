Clarity & Transparency

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers about how we protect your budget, timeline, and quality standards.

Before demolition or procurement begins, we furnish a comprehensive, itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ). Every material specification—from copper cable gauges and cement types to Italian marble varieties and sanitary fittings—is formally agreed upon and priced. We operate on structured milestone payments tied directly to verified site inspections, eliminating hidden surprise costs.

Over 40% of our clients reside in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Europe, or the Americas. We establish a dedicated WhatsApp management channel where our site engineers transmit weekly high-resolution video inspections, 360° progress captures, and digital milestone sign-off sheets. You have full transparency over every millimeter of execution without needing to travel to Cairo.

Our licensed civil engineers personally lead site inspections, conduct plumbing pressure testing, laser level alignments, and direct our vetted craftsman crews. We deliberately limit the number of active projects we accept each year to guarantee direct engineering oversight on every residence.

Interior decorators focus strictly on surface styling, often lacking technical training in electrical conduit loads, drainage slopes, waterproofing membranes, and structural load distributions. As licensed civil engineers and PMP® certified practitioners, the Heim team coordinates the technical skeleton and luxury finishes as one unified system, ensuring long-term structural integrity.

Upon final walkthrough and client sign-off, Heim issues a written workmanship warranty covering your concealed plumbing networks, electrical wiring systems, and waterproofing integrity. Should any technical issue arise post-handover, our team responds directly.
